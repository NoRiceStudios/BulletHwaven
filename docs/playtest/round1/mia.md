# Playtest round 1: Mia (persona 1, "the after-work survivor")

**Run:** solo, seed 101, driver MIA (paint 2, blue). Survived 8 full rounds and was eliminated at 0:57 in round 9 ("Your convoy is lost: ran dry in round 9"). 1050 kills, 9/15 tech.

**Build:** Machine Gun L2 and Scatter Gun L1 (third hardpoint bought, but never filled), Regenerator L3, Ablative Scrap, Steel Plates, Nitro Injection. Tech: Ballistics I–II, Engineering I–III, Propulsion I, Electronics I–II, Salvage I. Doctrine was circle/nearest for rounds 1–5, then kite/nearest after my third death.

**How it went:** Rounds 1–2 were a stroll (0 and 3 damage). From round 3 on, every round played out the same way: about 45 seconds of calm, then the screen filled with armoured boulders and acid spitters and I went from roughly 90% to 1% HP in the last 10 seconds. I died at 57–58 s in rounds 3, 4 and 5, each costing 20 fuel. Switching to KITE kept me alive in rounds 6 and 7. Round 8 killed me at 45 s and round 9 finished me off.

## Observations

- **The 0:57 death.** In rounds 3, 4, 5 and 9 I died between 57 and 58 seconds into a 60-second round, so three seconds later I would have been safe. It felt unfair, not like my mistake. HP sat at 87–100% at the 40 s mark every time, and the alive count went from about 50 to 110–176 in the last 15 seconds. As a Vampire Survivors player I expect a steady climb, not a cliff at the end of every round.
- **Nothing tells me why I died.** The workshop never mentions it. The only clue was the fuel can dropping from 100 to 80. I only found out it was "boulder contact" and acid because the harness told me. In the game I'd just see a big pile of rocks and a lost life.
- **CIRCLE drives me into the blob.** The round-5 frame shows my rig nosing straight into a clump of ~20 boulders. "Orbits the swarm" sounds safe but is suicidal against tanks. I switched to KITE on my own, and the game never nudged me. The KITE switch was the most satisfying decision of the run because it clearly worked: no deaths for two rounds.
- **Interest confused me.** I saved up to 411 scrap and it still said "Interest +5". So should I save or spend? I couldn't tell.
- **The third hardpoint was a let-down.** I bought "+1 weapon slot" for 300 scrap, the most expensive and exciting-sounding thing, and then the next draft never offered a weapon. The slot stayed empty and I died.
- **The workshop is lovely to look at.** Paper tags with level pips, a "LV 1 > 2" preview with before > after numbers, and the doctrine terminal with a one-line flavour description ("Keeps its distance"). Picking felt quick, the way I like it.
- **Small text oddity:** Vulture says "+25% dmg vs wounded" on the card. I wasn't sure whether "wounded" means below half HP or any damage at all.
- **The rig behind the cards:** in a few workshops my rig was parked right behind the card row or at the screen edge, so I barely saw it. The workshop is the moment I want to admire my rig.
- **The game-over screen is nice:** "Favourite part: Regenerator Lv 3" is a cute touch, and "Enter - restart" is right there.

## Top 5 issues (by impact)

1. **The end-of-round spike causes most deaths in the last seconds.** The alien count snowballs from ~50 to 110–176 in the final 15 s, and HP drops from ~90% to 0 almost at once. *Suggestion:* flatten the in-round spawn curve (cap boulders alive, spread spitter waves out), or start "Hive retreats" when the alive count passes a threshold. At minimum, a last-seconds buffer such as damage reduction in the final 5 s would stop the "died at 0:57" feeling.
2. **Deaths aren't explained and the doctrine fix is hidden.** *Suggestion:* add a "Last round" strip to the workshop, e.g. "Wrecked at 0:57 by boulder rams (64%), acid (30%)", and when one damage source dominates, highlight a doctrine hint ("Boulders ram you: try KITE"). This is exactly the nudge a casual player needs.
3. **The power curve doesn't keep up and the rig never changes.** Weapon damage rose about 50% while hive HP went up 120%, and the rig sprite looked the same throughout. *Suggestion:* scale weapon cards and tiers harder, add visible kit to the rig for each gun, armour and engine card, and add a "snowball" effect or two (chain hits, explosions on kill).
4. **Weapon drafts are thin.** Only MG and Scatter Gun appeared in 9 drafts, an empty third hardpoint went unfilled, and one draft was three mobility cards. *Suggestion:* guarantee at least one weapon offer whenever a gun slot is free, never offer three cards from the same category, and add more weapon types early.
5. **Late-round combat is hard to read.** Boulders merge into one textured mass, acid bolts are tiny, corpses look like live mites, and the HP bar has no number. *Suggestion:* add a stronger outline or shadow on boulders, make acid bolts bigger and brighter with a trail, fade corpses faster or desaturate them, add HP numbers plus a flash or vignette at low HP, and show current HP in the workshop.

## What I loved

- The art: the desert, the rusty scrap-welded rig and the cyan-cracked aliens look like a real indie release, and the title logo is great.
- The workshop is fast and tactile: three paper tags, number keys, and before/after numbers on every upgrade. No walls of text.
- The doctrine idea: one switch from CIRCLE to KITE changed my run, and that felt smart and low-effort.
- Fuel as lives is a clear, thematic stakes meter, and "Your convoy is lost / ran dry" is a great line.
- 25 s workshops and sub-minute rounds suit a 20-minute after-work session.
