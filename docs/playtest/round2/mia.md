# Playtest round 2: Mia (persona 1, "the after-work survivor")

**Run:** solo, seed 101, driver MIA (paint 2, blue). Survived 8 full rounds and was eliminated at 0:53 in round 9 ("Your convoy is lost: ran dry in round 9"). 1474 kills, 10/15 tech. That's the same round as last time, with about 40% more kills.

**Build:** Siege Cannon L2 (my favourite part, per the game-over screen), Machine Gun L1, Scatter Gun L1 (bought the third hardpoint, and this time a gun showed up straight away), Reinforced Hull L2, Steel Plates, Regenerator, Caterpillar Tracks. Tech: Ballistics I–III, Engineering I–III, Electronics I–II, Propulsion I–II. Doctrine: circle for rounds 1–4. Then the workshop hint said "Acid 65%: try CHARGE", so I charged in round 5 and died twice to boulders. The next hint said "Boulders 94%: try KITE", and I stayed on kite until the end. From then on the hint kept saying "Boulders even on kite: bolt on armour". I bolted on armour every round and still died every round.

**How it went:** Rounds 1–2 are now 40 s and I took 0 damage. They're short enough that the stroll doesn't bore me any more. Round 3 was a nice gentle first bite (85% HP left). Round 4 was fine. From round 5 on I lost one fuel can per round: wrecked at 0:44 and 1:00 (round 5), 0:59 (round 7), 0:49 (round 8) and 0:53 (round 9). Only round 6 was survived. Every death was a boulder ram (61–94% of damage taken), and every time the alive count climbed to 80–130 in the last 20 seconds while my HP dropped from about 75% to 0.

## What improved

- **The workshop tells me what happened.** "Wrecked at 0:59 by a boulder", a coloured damage-split bar (boulder rams, acid, mite bites), and "Fuel -20 lost". This was my #2 complaint, and it's fixed.
- **Doctrine nudges with a one-click button.** "Boulders 94%: try KITE [SET KITE]" is exactly the casual-friendly nudge I asked for.
- **Combat readability:** acid is now a big glowing cyan orb, boulders have dark outlines, there's an HP number (387/400), and the screen edges glow red at low HP.
- **Weapon variety:** Flamethrower and Siege Cannon were on offer in the very first draft, cards show "+1 GUN", and the cannon's level 2 added splash, which actually felt like an upgrade and not just a number.
- **Third hardpoint pays off:** right after buying it, the next draft offered two weapons.
- **Interest is clear:** "+13/13" shows the cap, so I know saving past it is pointless.
- **Rounds 1–2 are shorter (40 s),** so the safe warm-up doesn't drag.

## What still bugs me

- **I still die in the last 15 seconds of every round.** The alive count goes 50 → 80 → 130 in the final third, and boulders form a solid mass that rams me. Two deaths happened at 0:59 and 1:00. That's still the "so close!" unfair feeling.
- **The hints led me into a death spiral.** Acid hint → CHARGE → boulders killed me twice (−40 fuel). Then KITE → "boulders even on kite: bolt on armour" → I picked armour three rounds in a row and it still said the same thing. A hint that repeats itself after I follow it feels like the game shrugging.
- **No doctrine counters boulders.** KITE drove me into or along the boulder pile (round 7 frame: my rig is wedged against a 20-boulder clump at 100/600 HP).
- **The power curve lags behind the hive.** Kills per round flatlined at about 200 from round 5 on. My numbers grew, but it never snowballed the way a Vampire Survivors build does.
- **The workshop hides my rig.** It sat behind the banner (rounds 2 and 4), under the cards (round 6), or at the screen edge behind the tech panel (rounds 5 and 7).
- **Loadout HP shows only max HP,** and the game-over screen has no "cause of death" or round recap.

## Top 5 issues (by impact)

1. **The late-round spike still decides every death.** All 5 deaths came at 0:44–1:00, with 80–130 aliens alive. *Suggestion:* cap boulders alive (e.g. 8–10) and spawn the overflow as mites, or make the last 10 s a "hive retreats" wind-down where spawning stops. At minimum, add a 2–3 s grace at round end so a 0:59 death doesn't cost a fuel can.
2. **No working answer to boulders.** Charge suicides into them and Kite still gets cornered. *Suggestion:* teach KITE to steer away from the densest cluster rather than just the nearest enemy, and/or give boulders a telegraphed charge (glow plus wind-up) that the AI dodges. Offer an explicit anti-boulder card (e.g. "Ram Plow: boulders bounce off" or knockback rounds) when boulders are the main killer.
3. **Hints don't adapt and can backfire.** *Suggestion:* weigh hints against all damage sources (don't suggest CHARGE when boulders are present), and once I've followed a hint, escalate it to something concrete: "Still boulders: pick Ablative Scrap / try STRONGEST target / Caterpillar Tracks". Better still, highlight the matching card in the draft.
4. **The power fantasy plateaus.** Kills stayed flat at about 200 per round from round 5 to 9. *Suggestion:* give more cards L2/L3 "breakpoint" effects like the cannon's splash (MG ricochet, scatter knockback, regen burst on kill), and make weapon level-ups show up more often than flat +HP cards once a slot is filled.
5. **The workshop and game-over screens hide the rig and the story.** *Suggestion:* park the rig in a fixed, clear "garage" spot (e.g. the empty area under the doctrine panel or between the strip and the cards), drop the "Hive retreats" banner once the workshop opens, show current/max HP in the loadout, and add a one-line cause of death plus a mini round-by-round fuel chart to the game-over screen.

## What I loved

- The "Last round" strip with the damage-split bar and the **SET KITE** button. It's exactly what I wished for in round 1 and it reads in one glance.
- The Siege Cannon: big chunky number, splash at level 2, and it showed up as my "favourite part" at the end, which was a nice payoff.
- Big glowing acid orbs and the red low-HP vignette. I always knew when I was in trouble.
- The art is still the star. The burning wreck in a nest of cyan-cracked boulders on the game-over screen is a great final image.
- 40 s warm-up rounds, 60 s main rounds, 25 s workshops: it still fits a 20-minute after-work session.
