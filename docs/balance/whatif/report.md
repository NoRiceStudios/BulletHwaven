# Bot league balance report

Generated by `scripts/bot-league.sh` (`game/tools/bot_league.gd`, policies in `game/tools/bots.gd`).
520 matches: 440 solo rows, 80 ffa matches. Round cap per match: 20 (real timings, `fast_rounds = false`).

Caveats: bots are heuristic (see `bots.gd`), doctrine is static per policy, bots never react to rivals except `players_first`, results are for the sim as checked out when the run was made, and ffa win rates depend on the policy mix. Card/tech lift compares players that own an item at the start of round 5 against those that do not, and is only comparable within a column. `se` = standard error; |lift| > 2 se is flagged `*`.

## Findings

- **Upgrade value (solo):** `balanced` scores 14.1 vs 5.0 for `never_upgrade` (+9.1 rounds, x2.82).
- **Solo policy ranking (score):** best `balanced_kite` (16.2), `greedy_damage` (15.7), `balanced` (14.1), `tech_rush` (11.1); worst `focus_flamer` (8.0), `balanced_charge` (7.8), `random` (6.7), `never_upgrade` (5.0).
- **Dominant solo strategies (> balanced + 1.5 rounds):** `greedy_damage` (+1.6 vs balanced), `balanced_kite` (+2.1 vs balanced).
- **Solo strategies far behind balanced (< -4 rounds, excluding baselines):** `speedster` (-6.1), `focus_mg` (-5.2), `focus_shotgun` (-4.4), `focus_flamer` (-6.1), `saver` (-6.0), `balanced_charge` (-6.3), `pvp_hunter` (-5.9).
- **Pacing, solo:** 0 of 15 policies survive to the round cap in 50%+ of runs (-): the alien curve is too gentle for decent builds.
- **Pacing, solo:** eliminated runs end at median round 7 (p10 5, p90 12); a rig has 5 lives (20 fuel each), see the fuel table.
- **Weapon strength (solo score of the single-weapon focus policies):** `mg` 8.9, `shotgun` 9.7, `cannon` 10.9, `flamer` 8.0.
- **Economy:** a `balanced` rig ends with 2764 unspent scrap on average (all 15 tech cost 2650 in total): scrap is a real (if mild) constraint.
- **FFA policies winning significantly more than 25%:** `pvp_hunter` (83%, avg place 1.26).
- **FFA policies winning significantly less than 25%:** `never_upgrade` (0%, avg place 3.48), `greedy_damage` (0%, avg place 2.55), `focus_mg` (5%, avg place 2.76), `focus_shotgun` (4%, avg place 2.96), `balanced_charge` (0%, avg place 2.70).
- **Pacing, ffa:** 61% of matches are decided before the round cap; median length 16 rounds (p90 20).
- **PvP share:** 54% of ffa vehicle losses were caused by another player (bots only attack players when `players_first`).
- **Cards that significantly raise survival (random-policy lift, owned at round 5):** `mg` (+0.6 rounds), `cannon` (+1.2 rounds).
- **Cards that significantly lower it:** `scrap_magnet` (-0.5 rounds), `vulture` (-0.5 rounds).
- **Cards almost never picked by any scored policy (<10% pick rate):** none.
- **Tech that significantly raises survival (random-policy lift):** `ballistics_1` (+0.3 rounds), `ballistics_2` (+1.0 rounds), `engineering_1` (+0.6 rounds), `engineering_2` (+0.7 rounds).
- **Tech that significantly lowers it:** none detected.
- **Tech owned by fewer than 25% of players at the end:** `propulsion_3`, `electronics_3`, `salvage_3`.
