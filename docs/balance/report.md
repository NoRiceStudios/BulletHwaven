# Bot league balance report

Generated by `scripts/bot-league.sh` (`game/tools/bot_league.gd`, policies in `game/tools/bots.gd`).
520 matches: 440 solo rows, 80 ffa matches. Round cap per match: 20 (real timings, `fast_rounds = false`).

Caveats: bots are heuristic (see `bots.gd`), doctrine is static per policy, bots never react to rivals except `players_first`, results are for the sim as checked out when the run was made, and ffa win rates depend on the policy mix. Card/tech lift compares players that own an item at the start of round 5 against those that do not, and is only comparable within a column. `se` = standard error; |lift| > 2 se is flagged `*`.

## Findings

- **Upgrade value (solo):** `balanced` scores 19.4 vs 5.3 for `never_upgrade` (+14.1 rounds, x3.65).
- **Solo policy ranking (score):** best `balanced_kite` (20.2), `greedy_damage` (19.9), `balanced` (19.4), `tech_rush` (18.1); worst `speedster` (10.3), `pvp_hunter` (10.2), `random` (8.1), `never_upgrade` (5.3).
- **Dominant solo strategies (> balanced + 1.5 rounds):** none.
- **Solo strategies far behind balanced (< -4 rounds, excluding baselines):** `tank` (-7.2), `speedster` (-9.1), `focus_mg` (-4.8), `focus_shotgun` (-5.6), `focus_cannon` (-4.4), `focus_flamer` (-4.9), `saver` (-8.1), `pvp_hunter` (-9.2).
- **Pacing, solo:** 3 of 15 policies survive to the round cap in 50%+ of runs (greedy_damage, balanced, balanced_kite): the alien curve is too gentle for decent builds.
- **Pacing, solo:** eliminated runs end at median round 8 (p10 6, p90 15); a rig has 5 lives (20 fuel each), see the fuel table.
- **Weapon strength (solo score of the single-weapon focus policies):** `mg` 14.6, `shotgun` 13.8, `cannon` 15.0, `flamer` 14.5.
- **Economy:** a `balanced` rig ends with 9918 unspent scrap on average (all 15 tech cost 500 in total): scrap is never a constraint, so tech prices, reroll costs and interest do not matter.
- **FFA policies winning significantly more than 25%:** `pvp_hunter` (74%, avg place 1.39).
- **FFA policies winning significantly less than 25%:** `never_upgrade` (0%, avg place 3.59), `focus_mg` (5%, avg place 2.76), `focus_shotgun` (4%, avg place 3.09), `focus_cannon` (6%, avg place 2.06), `balanced_charge` (0%, avg place 2.04).
- **Pacing, ffa:** 52% of matches are decided before the round cap; median length 20 rounds (p90 20).
- **PvP share:** 65% of ffa vehicle losses were caused by another player (bots only attack players when `players_first`).
- **Cards that significantly raise survival (random-policy lift, owned at round 5):** `mg` (+0.9 rounds), `cannon` (+1.0 rounds).
- **Cards that significantly lower it:** `scrap_magnet` (-0.6 rounds), `vulture` (-0.9 rounds).
- **Cards almost never picked by any scored policy (<10% pick rate):** none.
- **Tech that significantly raises survival (random-policy lift):** `ballistics_1` (+1.5 rounds), `ballistics_2` (+1.5 rounds), `ballistics_3` (+1.9 rounds), `engineering_1` (+1.6 rounds), `engineering_2` (+1.9 rounds), `engineering_3` (+1.6 rounds), `propulsion_1` (+1.1 rounds), `propulsion_2` (+1.8 rounds), `propulsion_3` (+1.7 rounds), `electronics_1` (+1.2 rounds), `electronics_2` (+1.6 rounds), `electronics_3` (+1.8 rounds), `salvage_1` (+1.0 rounds), `salvage_2` (+1.1 rounds), `salvage_3` (+1.6 rounds).
- **Tech that significantly lowers it:** none detected.

## Proposed changes

Three runs with identical bots, seeds and settings (10 solo seeds per policy, 300 for `random`, 80 ffa matches, 20-round cap) are compared:

- **before**: `docs/balance/baseline/` (original numbers).
- **after**: this report (the one change that was applied, see below).
- **what-if**: `docs/balance/whatif/`, a throw-away copy of the project with the whole proposal package below (not applied; it touches `sim_world.gd` and a test).

Noise: with 10 seeds, differences below about 1 round (solo) or about 10 points of win rate (ffa) are within run-to-run noise. `balanced` moved 20.2 -> 19.4 between before and after although the shotgun is the only change (the shotgun is one of its weapons, and a card change reshuffles the random streams).

### Applied (number changes in `game/sim/cards.gd`)

| file | constant | old | new | rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `game/sim/cards.gd` | `WEAPONS["shotgun"]["damage"]` | `[3.0, 3.5, 4.0]` | `[3.5, 4.0, 4.5]` | The shotgun is the weakest weapon in every view: `focus_shotgun` scores 12.8 vs 14.5-15.0 for mg/flamer/cannon (-12 to -15%), has the worst ffa placement of the weapon specialists (avg place 3.17, 0% wins) and a random-policy lift of only +0.1 round (mg +1.2, cannon +1.0). Damage is the least intrusive lever (+17%, about 32 -> 37.5 dps at level 1 with all pellets hitting). |

Effect: `focus_shotgun` 12.8 -> 13.8 solo; ffa avg place 3.17 -> 3.08; random-policy lift +0.10 -> +0.25. Direction is right, size is near the noise floor; if the shotgun still lags in the next run, the range (150) is the next lever.
