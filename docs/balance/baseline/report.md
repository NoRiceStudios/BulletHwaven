# Bot league balance report

Generated by `scripts/bot-league.sh` (`game/tools/bot_league.gd`, policies in `game/tools/bots.gd`).
520 matches: 440 solo rows, 80 ffa matches. Round cap per match: 20 (real timings, `fast_rounds = false`).

Caveats: bots are heuristic (see `bots.gd`), doctrine is static per policy, bots never react to rivals except `players_first`, results are for the sim as checked out when the run was made, and ffa win rates depend on the policy mix. Card/tech lift compares players that own an item at the start of round 5 against those that do not, and is only comparable within a column. `se` = standard error; |lift| > 2 se is flagged `*`.

## Findings

- **Upgrade value (solo):** `balanced` scores 20.2 vs 5.3 for `never_upgrade` (+14.9 rounds, x3.82).
- **Solo policy ranking (score):** best `balanced_kite` (20.4), `balanced` (20.2), `greedy_damage` (20.1), `tech_rush` (18.2); worst `speedster` (10.3), `pvp_hunter` (10.1), `random` (8.1), `never_upgrade` (5.3).
- **Dominant solo strategies (> balanced + 1.5 rounds):** none.
- **Solo strategies far behind balanced (< -4 rounds, excluding baselines):** `tank` (-7.7), `speedster` (-9.9), `focus_mg` (-5.6), `focus_shotgun` (-7.4), `focus_cannon` (-5.2), `focus_flamer` (-5.7), `saver` (-9.3), `balanced_charge` (-5.1), `pvp_hunter` (-10.1).
- **Pacing, solo:** 3 of 15 policies survive to the round cap in 50%+ of runs (greedy_damage, balanced, balanced_kite): the alien curve is too gentle for decent builds.
- **Pacing, solo:** eliminated runs end at median round 8 (p10 6, p90 15); a rig has 5 lives (20 fuel each), see the fuel table.
- **Weapon strength (solo score of the single-weapon focus policies):** `mg` 14.6, `shotgun` 12.8, `cannon` 15.0, `flamer` 14.5.
- **Economy:** a `balanced` rig ends with 10657 unspent scrap on average (all 15 tech cost 500 in total): scrap is never a constraint, so tech prices, reroll costs and interest do not matter.
- **FFA policies winning significantly more than 25%:** `pvp_hunter` (74%, avg place 1.39).
- **FFA policies winning significantly less than 25%:** `never_upgrade` (0%, avg place 3.59), `focus_shotgun` (0%, avg place 3.17), `focus_cannon` (6%, avg place 1.97), `balanced_charge` (0%, avg place 2.13).
- **Pacing, ffa:** 51% of matches are decided before the round cap; median length 20 rounds (p90 20).
- **PvP share:** 64% of ffa vehicle losses were caused by another player (bots only attack players when `players_first`).
- **Cards that significantly raise survival (random-policy lift, owned at round 5):** `mg` (+1.2 rounds), `cannon` (+1.0 rounds).
- **Cards that significantly lower it:** `vulture` (-0.8 rounds).
- **Cards almost never picked by any scored policy (<10% pick rate):** none.
- **Tech that significantly raises survival (random-policy lift):** `ballistics_1` (+1.2 rounds), `ballistics_2` (+1.5 rounds), `ballistics_3` (+1.8 rounds), `engineering_1` (+1.5 rounds), `engineering_2` (+1.8 rounds), `engineering_3` (+1.8 rounds), `propulsion_1` (+1.1 rounds), `propulsion_2` (+1.7 rounds), `propulsion_3` (+1.4 rounds), `electronics_1` (+1.1 rounds), `electronics_2` (+1.6 rounds), `electronics_3` (+2.1 rounds), `salvage_1` (+1.0 rounds), `salvage_2` (+1.1 rounds), `salvage_3` (+1.8 rounds).
- **Tech that significantly lowers it:** none detected.
